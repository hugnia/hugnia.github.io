What do developers want AI assistants to do?

Which coding tasks do developers hand over to AI?

Our survey showed where developers welcomed help, including writing tests and documentation, and where limited project context or trust made them hesitate.

FSE 2026 Industry Track · Co-author

When can developers trust AI confidence signals?

General confidence scores did not reliably predict acceptance. Personalization helped when enough user data was available, and developers preferred color-coded indicators in the editor.

About

I came to software engineering research through clinical psychology. I’m interested in how people make decisions under uncertainty, where they direct their attention, and how they check an answer they get from a tool. My doctoral research at TU Delft focuses on human-centered AI in development environments.

Outside research, I play Dungeons & Dragons and enjoy traveling.
